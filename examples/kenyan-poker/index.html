Kenyan Poker / Kadi

Our table rules

Four cards each, 54-card deck. Match suit or rank. Draw once, then play or end your turn. Call Niko Kadi! at two cards, before playing down to one; forgetting costs one card. Finish on 4, 5, 6, 7, 9 or 10. A special final card draws a replacement.

2 / 3 / JokerPick 2 / 3 / 5. Stack the same rank or block with an Ace. Jokers match red or black suits.

AceChoose the next suit. Cancels a draw penalty.

Jack / KingSkip / reverse. Both give another turn with two players.

8 / QueenAsk a question: play again to answer, or draw and end your turn.

House rules vary across Kenya. This table uses single-card plays, immediate skips and suit-only Ace demands. Play is free.
